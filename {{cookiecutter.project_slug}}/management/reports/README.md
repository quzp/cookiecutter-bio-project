# management/reports/

Internal and periodic reporting — written on a schedule, to people funding or
supervising the work, as distinct from the manuscript written once for the field.

```
reports/
├── progress/         Periodic progress reports, YYYYMMDD_recipient.md
├── committee/        Thesis or advisory committee updates
├── grants/           Grant progress reports and renewal material for this project
└── slides/           Lab meeting and conference decks
```

Conventions:

- Name by date and audience: `20260601_r01_annual_progress.md`.
- Keep the submitted version; the numbers in it were true at that date even if the
  analysis has moved on since.
- Pull figures from `results/figures/` rather than from the manuscript — reports usually
  need the unassembled versions.
