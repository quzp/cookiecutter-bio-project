# management/

Everything involved in *running* the project rather than doing the science. Kept apart
from `src/`, `data/` and `manuscript/` so that the scientific record stays uncluttered.

| Subdirectory | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `config/` | Analysis parameters, thresholds, external paths — read by scripts |
| `docs/` | Literature notes, meeting minutes, method surveys, background reading |
| `reports/` | Progress reports, committee updates, grant reporting, lab meeting decks |

**`reports/` vs. `manuscript/`:** `reports/` is internal and periodic — written to a
schedule and to someone who is funding or supervising the work. `manuscript/` is written
once, to the field. They draw on the same results but have different audiences and
different lifetimes.
